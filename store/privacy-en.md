## In short

- No account, no tracking, no ads, no selling of data.
- Your dogs, reminders, saved places and conversations with the assistant are
  stored **only on your phone**. We cannot access them.
- We use your location only to search, while the app is open. We do not store it.

## What data, why, and on what basis

| Data | Purpose | Where it goes | Retention | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Location or typed address | Finding places nearby | Address lookup via OpenStreetMap Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation, UK). Place data itself is downloaded as files, without your location | Not by us. Nominatim keeps server logs briefly under its own policy | Providing what you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Questions to the AI assistant, plus (if you choose a dog) its breed, age, weight and sex | Giving an answer | Microsoft Azure OpenAI, in the EU. Not used to train models | Not stored by us. Microsoft may keep messages up to 30 days for abuse monitoring | Performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Random install ID and IP address | Limiting misuse of the assistant (maximum number of questions per 10 minutes) | Our server at Microsoft Azure (EU) | In memory only, at most 10 minutes | Legitimate interest: limiting abuse and costs (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Spoken questions | Converting speech to text | Your phone's speech recognition (Apple or Google), on the device where possible. **Snuffel never receives audio** | Under Apple's or Google's policy | Your choice to use the microphone |
| Your dog's photo, profiles, reminders, saved places, conversations | App features | Nowhere: they stay on your phone | Until you delete them or the app | n/a |

Reminders are notifications scheduled by your phone itself; there is no push
server.

## Map and place data

The map and place lists are loaded from our storage at Microsoft Azure (EU).
Like any web server it sees your IP address; we do not build profiles or
statistics from it. Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

## Children

Snuffel is not aimed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their
data.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection
and portability. As your data is on your phone, you can view and delete it
yourself (in the app, or by removing the app). For other requests:
**[E-MAIL ADDRESS]**. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection
Authority (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top. Important changes are
announced in the app.
