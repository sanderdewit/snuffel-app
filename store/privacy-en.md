## In short

- No account, no tracking, no ads, no selling of data.
- Your dogs, their files, reminders, saved places and conversations with the
  assistant are stored **only on your phone**. We cannot access them.
- We use your location only to search, while the app is open. We do not store it.

## What data, why, and on what basis

Reminders are notifications scheduled by your phone itself; there is no push
server.

## Backups, export and security

- **On your phone**, Snuffel's data is encrypted while the phone is locked
  (standard iOS and Android protection).
- **Backup:** the data is included in your phone's normal backup (iCloud or
  Google). You control those backups, under Apple's or Google's terms; we have
  no access to them.
- **Export:** you can export a dog's file as a PDF or as a file for another
  phone. You decide who you share it with. You can protect the file with a
  password (AES-256 encryption); without that password nobody can open it, us
  included.

## Map and place data

The map and place lists are loaded from our storage at Microsoft Azure (EU).
Like any web server it sees your IP address; we do not build profiles or
statistics from it. Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

## Website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Your language choice and
recent search results are kept in your own browser (localStorage) to make
searching faster; you can clear them in your browser. The website looks up
addresses via OpenStreetMap Nominatim, and place data outside our coverage via
OpenStreetMap's public Overpass service.

## Children

Snuffel is not aimed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their
data.
