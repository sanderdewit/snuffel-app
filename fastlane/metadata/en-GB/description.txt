Snuffel helps you find the best care for your dog, nearby and independent. Vets, animal hospitals, groomers, pet shops, trainers, dog hotels and off-lead areas across Europe, and we show which places belong to large chains.

EMERGENCY: AN OPEN VET IN ONE TAP
Open the Emergency tab (or long-press the app icon) and Snuffel immediately shows the vets and animal hospitals that are open now, nearest first. Closed practices are hidden, and if nothing nearby is open, Snuffel searches further. Call or start directions straight away. Your own vet is always on top.

INDEPENDENT OR CHAIN?
Many practices have been bought by large chains in recent years, often without changing their name. Snuffel recognises them from the chains' own clinic lists, their websites and OpenStreetMap, and shows independent practices first.

YOUR DOG, YOUR REMINDERS
Create a profile for your dog (breed, age, weight, photo) and get timely reminders for vaccinations, deworming, flea and tick treatment and the yearly check-up.

AI HELP WITH HEALTH AND BEHAVIOUR
Ask about symptoms or behaviour, like barking, anxiety, pulling on the lead or puppy training, typed or by voice. The assistant knows your dog's profile, helps you judge how urgent something is and suggests reward-based first steps. General information, not a substitute for a vet or behaviourist.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
Your saved places, your own vet and all places within 15 km of home also work offline.

PRIVACY
No account, no tracking, no ads. Your dogs, reminders, saved places and chats stay on your phone. Your location is only used to search and is not stored.
